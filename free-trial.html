Before you book

Is the trial lesson really free?

Yes. The first 30 minutes are free and there is no obligation to continue.

What happens in the trial lesson?

You meet Lucia, talk about your goals and try a little Italian together. She checks your level and suggests a plan: format, pace and materials.

Where does the trial take place?

Online on video, or in person in Prague 1, 2 or 3, usually in a quiet café. You choose in the form.

What happens after the trial?

If you want to continue, you choose a package of private lessons or the small group course. See the prices.
