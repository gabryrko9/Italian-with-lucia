Privacy

Short and plain: what Lucia stores about you, why, and what you can ask for.

Who is responsible

Italian with Lucia is the teaching practice of Lucia, a self-employed teacher in Prague, Czech Republic. Contact: lucia.scollo2000@gmail.com.

What is stored, and why

  • When you book a lesson or sign in: your name, email, level, what you want to learn Italian for, your time zone, and your lessons (dates, notes, homework, package). This is used only to organise and teach your lessons and to contact you about them.
  • When you email Lucia: your message and address, used to reply.
  • When you ask for a free trial lesson: what you write in the form (name, email, level, online or Prague, preferred times, time zone and why you are learning Italian), used only to arrange your trial lesson and reply to you. The form is delivered to Lucia's email by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co).
  • When you reserve a place in a group course: what you write in the form (name, email, level, time zone, preferred times and your message), used only to organise the groups and contact you about them. The form is delivered to Lucia's email by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co).
  • Payments: handled directly between you and Lucia (for example by Revolut or bank transfer). The website does not process payments.

The legal basis is your request to take lessons and, where a form has a consent box, your consent (the box you tick).

Who can see it

Only you (after signing in with your email) and Lucia. The data is stored with a database provider (Supabase) that works on Lucia's behalf. It is never sold and never used for advertising.

Cookies and other services

The website uses no advertising or tracking cookies. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not identify you: it only shows totals, such as how many people opened a page and where they came from. Fonts are hosted on this website, so no font service is contacted. An optional video preview comes from YouTube, which may receive your IP address when it loads, and signing in stores a small token in your browser so you stay signed in.

How long, and your rights

Data is kept while you are a student and for as long as needed afterwards for lesson records and accounting. You can ask at any time to see your data, correct it, or have it deleted by emailing lucia.scollo2000@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Czech data protection authority, the Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).

Last updated: 15 October 2026.
