Italian for companies and teams in Prague

Practical Italian courses for teams that work with Italian clients, suppliers or colleagues. One-to-one or small groups, online or at your office in Prague, built on the situations your people actually face: meetings, calls, emails, trade fairs and business trips.

Why Italian, if everyone speaks English?

Business with Italy runs on relationships. A team that can greet a client, make small talk over coffee and read an email in Italian builds trust faster than one that only speaks English. Lucia teaches the Italian your people will actually use at work, and the business culture behind it.

Who it is for

  • Sales and export teams selling to Italian clients or distributors.
  • Purchasing and supply chain working with Italian suppliers and factories.
  • Managers and staff with Italian colleagues, owners or a head office in Italy.
  • Professionals preparing a trip, project or trade fair in Italy who need focused Italian in a few weeks.

How it works

  1. Free 30-minute call with HR or the team lead: goals, number of people, schedule.
  2. Level check for every participant, free of charge.
  3. Written proposal with groups, schedule, programme and price.
  4. Weekly lessons, online or at your office, with materials built on your sector.
  5. Progress report for HR every 10 lessons, and a certificate of attendance at the end.

Prices for companies

Every lesson lasts 60 minutes. Group prices are for the whole group, not per person. Courses start from 10 lessons.

Questions

Our team has different levels. Does it still work?

Yes. After the level check Lucia suggests how to split the team: small groups by level, or one-to-one lessons for people who are far ahead or behind.

Can lessons take place at our office?

Yes, anywhere in Prague, for €5 more per lesson. Lessons can also be online, or a mix of both.

What do the lessons cover?

The situations your team actually faces: introductions and small talk, meetings and calls, emails, negotiations, trade fairs and business trips, with the vocabulary of your sector and tips on Italian business culture.

How much does an Italian course for a team cost?

Every lesson lasts 60 minutes. One-to-one lessons cost €35, small groups of 2–4 people €60 per lesson, and team groups of 5–8 people €75 per lesson, for the whole group. Lessons at your office in Prague cost €5 more. Courses start from 10 lessons.
