A real teacher, a real conversation

Apps are great for vocabulary, but they cannot hear your pronunciation, answer your questions or notice the mistake you keep repeating. In an online lesson with Lucia, you speak Italian from the first minutes and get feedback on the spot. Most of Lucia's lessons happen online, so the format is smooth and well tested.

How online lessons work

  • Live video, one-to-one, 60 minutes per lesson.
  • A shared whiteboard for grammar, new words and examples, so you see everything as it is explained.
  • Recorded materials and homework between lessons, so you can review at your own pace.
  • Scheduling around your time zone. Tell Lucia where you live and when you are free.

Lessons are taught for English speakers. You can ask in English whenever something is unclear, and Lucia moves into Italian as soon as you are ready.

What you need

  • A laptop, tablet or phone with a camera and microphone.
  • A stable internet connection and, if possible, headphones.
  • A quiet corner for 60 minutes.

Who online lessons are for

Online lessons suit people in Prague who prefer to learn from home, expats who travel, and students living in other countries. If you live in Prague you can also meet Lucia in person: see Italian lessons in Prague. You can mix both formats.

Levels and goals

Lucia teaches every level, from absolute beginners to advanced learners. Lessons can focus on everyday conversation, grammar and pronunciation, Italian for work, or preparing a trip. New to Italian? Read about Italian for beginners.

Questions

Are online Italian lessons as good as in person?

For most learners, yes. You get the same one-to-one attention, live corrections and materials. A shared whiteboard makes grammar easy to follow, and lessons are never a pre-recorded video.

Can I take lessons from a different country or time zone?

Yes. Tell Lucia your time zone when you write, and she proposes times that work for you.

What do I need for an online lesson?

A device with camera and microphone, a stable internet connection and a quiet place. Headphones help.

How do I book the free trial lesson?

Send Lucia a short email at lucia.scollo2000@gmail.com with your level, goals and time zone. She replies within 24 hours.
