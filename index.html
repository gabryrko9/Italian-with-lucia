Questions

Do I need to know any Italian already?

No. Lessons are adapted to your level, from complete beginner to advanced. You build a strong foundation and your confidence from the first lesson.

Are the lessons online or in person?

Both. Learn online from anywhere in the world, or meet Lucia in person in Prague 1, 2 and 3.

How do I book the free trial?

Send Lucia a short email with your level and goals. She replies within 24 hours to agree a time. The trial lasts 30 minutes: you meet, she checks your level and you talk about your plan. There is no obligation to continue.

How do the group courses work?

Courses start in January: 12 weekly live lessons in a group of 3 to 5 students, at beginner, intermediate or advanced level. Reserve a place by email and Lucia confirms the day and time.

Which coursebook do I need?

Lessons follow the Nuovo Espresso series. Wait for your free trial and Lucia will tell you which level to buy.

Can lessons focus on my own goals?

Yes. Private lessons can be shaped around work, travel or exam goals.

How do I book my lessons and see them afterwards?

After your free trial, you book each lesson with Lucia and receive a confirmation for every booking: keep it as your record. Lucia keeps all bookings in her calendar. Group lesson times are confirmed by Lucia when you reserve a place.

Can I cancel a lesson?

Yes, up to 24 hours before the lesson starts: write to Lucia by email. After that the lesson cannot be cancelled and counts as taken. The same rule applies to private and group lessons.

How do I pay?

Revolut is preferred, and bank transfer works too. You can choose a 5, 10 or 20-lesson package, or a group course.
