Le domande — the questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to already speak Italian? +

No. Lessons adapt to your level, from complete beginner to advanced. We build a strong foundation — and confidence — from the very first lesson.

How does the free trial work? +

It's a relaxed 30-minute session: you meet Lucia, she evaluates your current level, and together you discuss your goals. No obligation to continue.

How do the winter group courses work? +

Each course runs from January to March: 12 weekly live lessons in a small group of 3–5 students, at beginner, intermediate or advanced level. You'll get the exact weekly time slot before enrolling.

How do payments work? +

Via Revolut or bank transfer. You can pay in EUR, CZK, or USD based on current exchange rates. Choose a 5-lesson or 10-lesson private package, or enrol in a group course.
