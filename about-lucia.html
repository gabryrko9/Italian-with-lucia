About Lucia

Lucia is an Italian teacher based in Prague. Italian with Lucia is her own teaching practice: private lessons and small group courses for English speakers, in person in Prague or online.

Email Lucia for a free trial See prices

Free, 30 minutes, no obligation. Lucia replies within 24 hours.

Who teaches your lessons

Every lesson at Italian with Lucia is taught by Lucia herself. She holds a Master's degree from the University for Foreigners of Perugia, a university that specialises in teaching Italian to people from other countries, and she has more than 1,000 hours of classroom experience with learners from complete beginners to advanced.

She teaches English speakers who want to speak, not just study: precise pronunciation, grammar that makes sense, and the confidence to express yourself. Every mistake is a step towards your new Italian voice.

How Lucia teaches

  • Speaking from minute one. You use Italian in every lesson, and you are corrected kindly.
  • Clear grammar. Rules are explained in English when you need it and in Italian as soon as you are ready.
  • A real path, made for you. Every lesson is shaped around your needs and goals. The Nuovo Espresso coursebook series is optional, with extra material chosen for you.
  • Homework and notes. After each lesson you find notes and homework in your student area.

Where and how

Lessons are online, or in person in Prague 1, 2 and 3. You can switch between the two whenever you like. See Italian lessons in Prague, online Italian lessons and the group course.

The first step is free: a 30-minute trial lesson, to meet, check your level and agree a plan. There is no obligation.
