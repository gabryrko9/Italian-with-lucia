Learn Italian in Prague, with a teacher who adapts to you

Prague is full of people who want to speak Italian: expats with an Italian partner, professionals who work with Italian clients, travellers planning a long trip to Italy, and students who simply love the language. Lucia teaches all of them, from complete beginners to advanced learners, and every lesson is built around your level and your goals.

Lessons are one-to-one and last 60 minutes. You speak from the first minute, and the grammar is explained clearly so it finally makes sense. Lessons are taught for English speakers, so explanations are in English whenever you need them and in Italian as soon as you are ready.

Who these lessons are for

  • Expats and long-term residents who want to follow Italian conversations, films and food culture.
  • People with an Italian partner or family who want to talk to them, and to their relatives, in Italian.
  • Professionals who deal with Italian colleagues or clients and need the right vocabulary and etiquette.
  • Travellers and future residents of Italy who want to handle real situations: restaurants, trains, flats, paperwork.
  • Learners who feel stuck and want precise feedback on pronunciation and grammar.

What a lesson looks like

Every lesson follows a simple rhythm, so you always know where you are:

  • A short warm-up conversation, to get you thinking in Italian.
  • One clear focus: a grammar point, a topic or a situation you actually need.
  • Guided speaking practice, with corrections that stay friendly.
  • Homework and materials between classes, so progress continues during the week.

Every lesson is shaped around your needs and goals. The Nuovo Espresso coursebook series is optional: use it if you like learning with a book, or rely on the materials Lucia chooses for you. You can decide after the free trial.

In person in Prague 1, 2 and 3, or online

In-person lessons take place in Prague 1, 2 and 3, in cafés or other meeting places we agree on. It works well if you live or work in the centre and learn best face to face. Prefer the sofa, or travelling for work? Every lesson also works online, with live video, a shared whiteboard and recorded materials. You can switch at any time. Questions

Where do the in-person lessons in Prague take place?

In Prague 1, 2 and 3, in cafés or other meeting places we agree on. Lucia confirms the exact place when you book. If you prefer, every lesson also works online.

Do I need to know any Italian already?

No. Lucia teaches complete beginners as well as advanced learners, and adapts every lesson to your level.

Can I switch between Prague and online?

Yes. Many students meet in person some weeks and join online when they travel or are busy.

How much do Italian lessons in Prague cost?

The first 30-minute trial is free. After that, lessons cost €30 each in a 5-lesson package, €27 each in a 10-lesson package and €25 each in a 20-lesson package.
