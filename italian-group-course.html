What you learn in 12 weeks

Each level has a clear plan, and Lucia shapes it around the people in the group: if everyone needs more speaking, or more help with one topic, the lessons follow that.

Beginner (A1–A2)

Start from zero, or restart after a long break. Good pronunciation from day one.

  • Introduce yourself, talk about where you live, your work and your family.
  • Order in a café or restaurant, shop, ask for prices and directions.
  • Numbers, days, times and dates, so you can make plans.
  • The present tense, articles, gender and the most useful everyday verbs.
  • Your first steps into the past, to say what you did at the weekend.

New to Italian? Read Italian for beginners and the essential phrases for travel.

Intermediate (B1)

Get past the plateau and speak with more confidence.

  • Past tenses that finally click: passato prossimo vs imperfetto.
  • Talk about plans, wishes and advice with the future and the conditional.
  • Pronouns, ci and ne, so your sentences sound natural.
  • Tell stories and give your opinion on real topics, every week.

Advanced (B2–C1)

Sharpen nuance, idioms and register.

  • The subjunctive where Italians actually use it.
  • Formal and informal Italian, for work, emails and everyday life.
  • Debate, argue your point and discuss articles, films and the news.
  • Precise feedback on the mistakes you still make.

How a group lesson works

  • A live video lesson: you receive the link before each class.
  • A short warm-up conversation, then one clear focus for the day.
  • Speaking in pairs and as a group, with friendly corrections from Lucia.
  • Notes and optional homework after the lesson, so you keep going during the week.

You do not have to buy a coursebook. Lessons can follow Nuovo Espresso (ALMA Edizioni) if you like having a book, but everything you need comes from Lucia.

Who it is for

  • People in Prague who want a weekly Italian class without travelling across the city.
  • Learners anywhere in Europe: lessons follow Prague time (CET), with the time set together with the group.
  • Anyone preparing for a holiday, a move to Italy, or talking with an Italian partner, friends or family.
  • Friends or couples who want to learn together: reserve together and say so in your email.

Not sure which level you are?

The level finder on the homepage takes ten seconds. Lucia also confirms your level before you join, so you end up in the right group. If you prefer to talk first, book the free 30-minute trial.

How to reserve your place

  1. Email Lucia with the level you want, your time zone and the days and times that suit you.
  2. Lucia confirms your level and the day and time of your group. Places are limited to five students per group.
  3. Pay for the course by Revolut (preferred) or bank transfer, only after your group is confirmed.

Group lessons follow the same 24-hour cancellation rule as private lessons: you can cancel up to 24 hours before a lesson, after that it counts as taken.

Group or private?

Choose the group if you like learning with others, want a steady weekly rhythm and prefer the lower price. Choose private lessons if you need your own pace, a specific goal such as work or an exam, or a flexible schedule. You can also combine both.

Questions

When does the group course start?

The group course starts in January. Lucia confirms the exact day and time of your group with you when you reserve a place.

How many students are in a group?

Three to five students, so everyone speaks in every lesson.

How much does the Italian group course cost?

€18 per person per lesson, which is €216 for the 12 weekly lessons.

When do I pay?

Only after Lucia confirms your group, its day and time. Reserving a place is free. Payment is by Revolut (preferred) or bank transfer.

What happens if not enough people join my level?

A group starts with at least three students. Since you pay only after your group is confirmed, you never pay for a group that does not start. If your level does not fill up, Lucia tells you and you decide together what to do next.

Do I need to buy a coursebook?

No. The Nuovo Espresso coursebook is optional: Lucia provides the materials, and you can add the book if you like learning with one.

Is the group course online?

Yes. The lessons are live online, so you can join from Prague or from anywhere.

What if I do not know my level?

Use the level finder on the homepage, or tell Lucia what you can do in Italian. She confirms your level before you join.
