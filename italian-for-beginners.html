Italian for beginners

Start from zero, with a teacher who makes grammar make sense. In your first lessons you learn to say hello, introduce yourself, order a coffee and be understood, with good pronunciation from day one.

You can start with no Italian at all

Many students start with Lucia knowing only "ciao" and "pizza". That is a perfectly good starting point. Lessons are built around you: if you like to speak, you speak; if you like to understand the rules first, Lucia explains them clearly and then makes you use them.

What you learn first

  • Greetings and introductions: saying hello, your name, where you are from.
  • Everyday situations: ordering a coffee, shopping, asking for directions.
  • Numbers, days and time, so you can make plans.
  • The present tense of the most useful verbs.
  • Pronunciation: Italian is written the way it sounds, once you know a few simple rules.

Your first Italian phrases

Buongiorno Good morning bwon-JOR-no

Mi chiamo Anna. My name is Anna. mee KYAH-mo

Piacere! Nice to meet you! pya-CHEH-re

Un caffè, per favore. A coffee, please. oon kaf-FEH

Dov'è la stazione? Where is the station? do-VEH la sta-TSYO-ne

Non capisco. I do not understand. non ka-PEE-sko

How long until I can have a simple conversation?

It depends on how often you study. As a general guide, one lesson a week plus a little practice every day helps most learners reach simple everyday conversation within a few months. Read the realistic answer in how long it takes to learn Italian.

Questions

Can I learn Italian with no previous knowledge?

Yes. Lucia teaches absolute beginners and adapts the pace to you.

Is Italian hard for English speakers?

Italian is one of the easier languages for English speakers: many words are similar, and spelling follows the sound. Verbs and gender need practice, and a teacher helps you avoid common mistakes early.

Do I need a coursebook?

No. The coursebook is optional: lessons are shaped around your needs and goals. If you like learning with a book, they can follow the Nuovo Espresso series, and Lucia tells you which volume fits after the free trial.

Can I start online?

Yes. Most beginners start online, and you can meet in person in Prague 1, 2 or 3 if you prefer.
